Airsports for Microsoft Flight Simulator, version {VERSION}
=============================================================

Track your simulator flights in Airsports Live Tracking contests: the client sends the aircraft's position to Airsports,
shows your flight, start timer, training feedback and photos, and puts a flight display in the MSFS toolbar.
Works with MSFS 2020 and MSFS 2024. You need a free Airsports account (create it in the client).


1. The client
-------------
Unzip this folder anywhere you like (for example Documents\Airsports), open "Airsports client" and start msfsclient.exe.
Nothing has to be installed; to remove it, delete the folder.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Sign in (or create an account), then press play at the bottom before your tracking window opens. Start MSFS and load a
flight; the client connects to the simulator by itself.


2. The toolbar panel in MSFS (optional)
---------------------------------------
Easiest: in the client, open your profile (your picture at the top) and press "Install the panel" under
"Toolbar panel in MSFS". Then restart the simulator.

By hand: copy the folder "airsports-flight-display" from the "Community" folder in this zip into your MSFS Community
folder, then restart the simulator.

In MSFS, open "Airsports" from the toolbar at the top of the screen. The panel needs the client running on the same PC.


Second screen
-------------
The same flight display is at http://localhost:47911/ in any browser on this PC while the client runs.
