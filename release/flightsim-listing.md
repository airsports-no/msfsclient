## Short description (one line)
Fly real air sports competitions in MSFS: air navigation races and precision flying, with a live map, automatic scoring
and a toolbar panel. Free.

## Description

**Turn your VFR flying into a competition.** [Air Sports Live Tracking](https://airsports.no) is a free competition
platform built for real-world air sports: air navigation races, precision flying and other navigation tasks, with live
maps and automatic scoring. Real pilots in real aircraft fly its competitions with the Air Sports app on their phones.
With this download you fly the same tasks in Microsoft Flight Simulator, on the same live map, scored by the same rules.
Fly the practice flight on your own today, join a contest, or organize one for your friends, club or virtual airline.

### What is an Air Navigation Race?
In an Air Navigation Race (ANR) the route is drawn on the map as a narrow corridor. You fly it at the speed you planned,
cross each gate at the planned second and stay inside the corridor the whole way. Every second early or late costs
points, and so does leaving the corridor. It is fast, it is hard, and on the live map everyone can watch you do it.

### What is precision flying?
Precision flying is navigation by map, clock and compass. You plan a route of turning points from the flight order and
fly it at a fixed speed. You are timed at every turning point, and at secret checkpoints along the route that you do not
know about in advance. Along the way you look out for ground features shown on photographs and mark them when you see
them. The best pilots are within a second or two at every gate. Real competitions add precision landings; the
simulator tasks cover the navigation.

### What you get
- **The Airsports client for Windows:** sign in, see the flight you are booked for, press play and fly. Your aircraft's
  position goes to Air Sports Live Tracking every second, so you appear on the live map and are scored like everybody
  else.
- **A practice flight for your first time:** press "Go flying" and the client registers you, opens your flight order
  and starts tracking. It can even put your aircraft on the runway or in the air before the start.
- **A toolbar panel in MSFS:** tracking on/off, the start timer, how early or late you were at each gate, and the photos
  to look for, without leaving the cockpit.
- **Training mode:** after every gate you see how many seconds early or late you were, so you learn as you fly.
- **Messages on the simulator's screen:** tracking started, positions received, the start minute, gate results.
- **Flight orders, a live map you can share with friends, your track history (GPX export) and results.**

### Your first flight, in five minutes
1. Install the client (see Installation) and sign in with Google, Apple or a free Air Sports account.
2. On Home, the card "New here? Try a practice flight" tells you where to start. Press **Go flying**.
3. Your flight order opens in the browser and tracking starts. Begin a flight in MSFS at the start location, or let the
   client move your aircraft there.
4. Use a weather preset without wind, take off, and cross the starting line. Then try to hit every gate on time.
5. Land, press stop, and see your flight and score on the live map.

### Regular flights
1. Find a task: "Register today" in the client lists the tasks open for registration, nearest to your aircraft first.
   You can also register on airsports.no, or the organizer can add you.
2. Choose your start time, speed and aircraft, and download the flight order to plan your flight.
3. On the day, run the client's pre-flight check, set up MSFS the way the organizer asks, and press play before your
   tracking window opens.
4. Fly the task. Use the start timer for an adaptive start and mark the photos as you find them.
5. Follow the live map and the results on airsports.no.

### Organize your own virtual competition
Anyone can set up a contest on airsports.no, and it is free to create one. Draw the route in the route editor, pick ANR,
precision flying or another task type, set the time window and let pilots register themselves. Air Sports Live
Tracking does the rest: flight orders, live tracking, automatic scoring and results.
- **Club nights and leagues:** a new task every week, flown whenever it suits each pilot.
- **Head-to-head races:** everyone on the same live map at the same time.
- **Training for the real thing:** fly a real competition's route in the simulator before the event.
- **Mixed events:** simulator pilots and real aircraft on the same route.

Set one up, share the link and see who is the best navigator in your group.

### Fly the real thing
Air Sports Live Tracking was built for real pilots in real aircraft, and the simulator joins that same platform.
Everything you practise in MSFS works the same way in the air.
- **Watch real competitions live** on [airsports.no](https://airsports.no): the live maps show every pilot's track,
  timing and score as they fly, and the results are there afterwards.
- **Train for a real event in the simulator:** fly its route in MSFS first, with the same flight order and the same
  scoring, so the real flight holds no surprises.
- **Take part yourself:** with a licence and an aircraft, the Air Sports app tracks you on your phone
  ([Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=no.airsports.android.livetracking),
  [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/air-sports-live-tracking/id1559193686)). The account you use in the simulator works
  there too.
- **Organizers:** a club can run its real competitions and a simulator league on the same site, with the same tools.

Visit [airsports.no](https://airsports.no) for contests open for registration, live maps, results, the route editor and
everything about the platform.

## Installation
1. Unzip anywhere, for example to Documents\Airsports, and start `Airsports client\msfsclient.exe`. There is nothing
   else to install. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. Optional: the toolbar panel. In the client, open your profile (picture or initials at the top right) and press
   "Install the panel". You can also copy `Community\airsports-flight-display` into your MSFS Community folder.
   Restart MSFS and open "Airsports" from the toolbar. The panel needs the client running on the same PC.
3. Sign in with Google, Apple or a free Air Sports account, and follow the README for the first flight.

The README in the zip covers installation, setup, the first flight, regular contest flights and troubleshooting.

## Requirements
- Windows 10 (1809 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit
- MSFS 2020 or MSFS 2024 (Microsoft Store or Steam)
- An internet connection while flying
- A Google or Apple account, or a free Air Sports Live Tracking account (create it in the client)
