## Short description (one line)
Track your flight live in Airsports contests: start timer, gate feedback, photo observations and a toolbar panel in the cockpit.

## Description
Airsports Live Tracking (airsports.no) runs air sports competitions for real pilots: air navigation races, precision and
rally flying, with live maps and automatic scoring. This download lets you fly them in Microsoft Flight Simulator.

**What you get**
- The Airsports client: sign in, see the flight you are scheduled for, and press play. Your aircraft's position goes to
  Airsports every second, so you appear on the live map and are scored like everybody else.
- A toolbar panel in MSFS: tracking on/off, the adaptive-start timer, how early or late you were at each gate (training
  mode), photo observations with a Mark button, and messages from the organizers, without leaving the cockpit.
- Short messages on the simulator's screen: tracking started, positions reaching the server, the start minute, gate
  results.
- Your own track history, exportable as GPX.

**How to fly a contest**
1. Create a free account in the client (or use your airsports.no account).
2. Register for a task on the website or with "Register today" in the client.
3. Load your flight in MSFS, press play in the client before your tracking window opens, and fly the route.

## Installation
1. Unzip anywhere (for example Documents\Airsports) and start `Airsports client\msfsclient.exe`. Nothing else to install.
   The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. Toolbar panel: in the client, open your profile (picture at the top) and press "Install the panel"; or copy
   `Community\airsports-flight-display` into your MSFS Community folder. Restart the simulator and open "Airsports" from
   the toolbar.

The panel needs the client running on the same PC.

## Requirements
- Windows 10 (1809 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit
- MSFS 2020 or MSFS 2024 (Microsoft Store or Steam)
- An internet connection while flying

## Also available
The client is also in the Microsoft Store (automatic updates); it installs the toolbar panel from its profile page.
